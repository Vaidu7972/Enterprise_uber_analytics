CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ AND POLICIES

Q: How do fare estimates work?
A: Fares are calculated using base fare, trip distance, duration, weather factors, and peak hour multipliers.

Q: What happens if an item is lost during a ride?
A: Riders can report lost items directly via the support tab. Drivers will be contacted by support to return items safely.

Q: How can riders report driver misconduct?
A: Safety concerns and driver misconduct can be reported immediately in the app or via the support escalation portal. Severe safety reports trigger immediate investigation.
