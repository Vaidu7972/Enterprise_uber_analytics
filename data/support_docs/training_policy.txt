DRIVER TRAINING AND COACHING SOP

When a driver underperforms or receives compliance warnings, operations assigns recommended coaching modules:
1. Customer Service & Hospitality Module (for ratings under 4.5)
2. Safe Driving & Navigation Module (for route delays or safety reports)
3. Platform Compliance & Cancellation SOP (for high cancellation rates)

Coaching courses must be completed within 7 days of assignment.
Upon completion, the driver's profile status is updated and monitored by the supervisor agent.
