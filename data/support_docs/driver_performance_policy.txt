DRIVER PERFORMANCE AND UNDERPERFORMANCE POLICY

Drivers are evaluated on key metrics including overall passenger rating, trip completion rate, monthly trip volume, and customer feedback.

A driver with a rating below 4.5 or high cancellation rates is flagged for underperformance risk.

When a driver is identified as underperforming:
1. Operations reviews recent trip logs and customer ratings.
2. If driver risk level is Medium or High, mandatory refresher driver training is assigned.
3. Drivers are monitored for 14 days following training.
4. Continued underperformance may lead to temporary account suspension or formal manual review.
